<div class="prose">
	<p>Broken Oath is a hack and slash inspired by Frank Miller's 300, with a twist.</p>
	<p>It borrows sprites from here and there, which are then harmonized through color changes, post-processing and color grading.</p>
</div>

<figure>
	<img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/img/broken-oath/intro.png" alt="Intro slide"/>
	<figcaption>Intro</figcaption>
</figure>

<div class="prose">
	<p>You chain combos with your sword, roll out of trouble, and block or parry to boost your next attack.</p>
	<p>It's got a proper intro and outro, a map, seven levels, three fight arenas, four enemy types ready to go for you, and a boss waiting at the end.</p>
</div>
