<div class="prose">
	<p>A short RPG-like game inspired by a trip to Peru. It makes use of particle effects and FMOD Studio.</p>
	<p>Footsteps are synced through Unity's animation events, and the sound variation is defined within the FMOD project. I found that keeping your sound project independent from the main Unity project greatly improves your workflow.</p>
	<p>For color grading it uses Unity's Post Processing Stack.</p>
