	<h2>FAQ</h2>

	<h3>How do I play?</h3>
	<p>Touch the left half of the screen to steer left and the right half to steer right. Dodge the asteroids and complete the goals of each mission to unlock the next one.</p>

	<h3>Where is my progress saved?</h3>
	<p>Only on your device. It isn't uploaded anywhere, so it is lost if you delete the app, and it doesn't carry over to other devices.</p>

	<h3>How do I reset my progress?</h3>
	<p>Delete the app and install it again from the App Store. It will start from the first mission.</p>

	<h3>Are there in-app purchases or ads?</h3>
	<p>No. Voyage has no in-app purchases and no ads.</p>

	<h2>Compatibility</h2>
	<p>Voyage runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 15.0 or later.</p>
